## III. DECISION-MAKING & GOVERNANCE

### Board Meetings
- **Frequency:** Quarterly (4 per year)
- **Attendees:** Board members + CEO + CFO
- **Agenda:** Financial review, product updates, fundraising, strategic initiatives
- **Minutes:** Formal documentation required
- **Voting:** Majority rule, ties go to CEO

### Executive Steering Committee
- **Members:** CEO, CTO, CFO, CRO
- **Frequency:** Bi-weekly
- **Focus:** Cross-functional alignment, tactical execution, weekly OKRs
- **Decisions:** Budget allocation, hiring, product priorities

### Product Council
- **Members:** VP Product, VP Engineering, VP Marketing, Community Lead
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Focus:** Feature prioritization, release planning, roadmap
- **Authority:** Can make decisions up to 2-week effort

### Decision Authority Matrix

| Decision | Authority | Approval | Threshold |
|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| **Budget <$50k** | VP level | CFO | Finance only |
| **Budget $50k-$500k** | EVP level | CEO + CFO | Finance + Strategy |
| **Budget >$500k** | Board | CEO | Board vote |
| **Hiring (IC)** | VP | CFO (budget) | Budget + role approval |
| **Hiring (Manager+)** | SVP | CEO | CEO approval |
| **Product Features** | VP Product | CTO | Technical feasibility |
| **Strategic Partnerships** | CEO | Board (if >$1M) | Strategic fit + legal |
| **Security/Privacy Policy** | CTO | CEO/Legal | Legal compliance |
| **Customer Refunds >$50k** | CRO | CEO | Customer value |

### Conflict of Interest Policy
- All board members and executives must disclose interests
- Recusal from discussions involving personal financial benefit
- Annual conflict of interest certification
- Outside board positions limited to 1 per employee

## IV. FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT

### Financial Planning & Budgeting
- **Budget Cycle:** Annual (Dec-Jan) + quarterly reviews
- **Planning Horizon:** 3-year strategic, 1-year operating
- **Review Process:** Monthly P&L review by CFO, quarterly by board
- **Variance Analysis:** >10% variance triggers investigation

### Financial Controls
- **Approval Authority:**
  - <$5k: Department manager approval
  - $5k-$50k: VP approval + CFO review
  - $50k-$500k: CFO + CEO approval
  - >$500k: CEO + Board approval

- **Expense Policies:**
  - Travel: Coach on flights <6 hours, business on longer
  - Meals: $50/day limit (discretionary +25% if client/partnership)
  - Conferences: Pre-approval required, max $5k/person/year
  - Equipment: $3k limit for standard tech setup

### Financial Reporting
- **Monthly:** Executive summary, P&L, cash flow, burn rate
- **Quarterly:** Full board financials, metrics dashboard, forecast
- **Annually:** Audited financials (post-Series A), tax filing

### Fundraising & Banking
- **Primary Bank:** Silicon Valley Bank (or equivalent)
- **Credit Facility:** $500k line of credit (Series A)
- **Cash Management:** CFO discretion up to $50k, CEO approval above
- **Investor Relations:** CEO primary, CFO secondary

## V. HUMAN RESOURCES & CULTURE

### Hiring & Onboarding
- **Process:** 
  1. Manager -> VP -> CEO approval (engineering/leadership)
  2. Source through network, LinkedIn, referrals
  3. Technical interviews (2-3 rounds)
  4. Culture/team fit round
  5. Reference checks
  6. Offer approval by CEO

- **Onboarding:**
  - 1-week: Tech setup, systems access, company overview
  - 1-month: Team integration, first project assignment
  - 3-month: Goal setting, manager 1-on-1s
  - 6-month: Performance review

### Compensation & Benefits
- **Salary:**
  - Benchmarked against San Francisco market (50th-75th percentile)
  - Annual review + merit increases (2-5%)
  - Salary bands by level, no negotiations post-offer

- **Equity:**
  - All employees (manager+) receive options
  - Vesting schedule: 4 years, 1-year cliff
  - Strike price: Fair Market Value (FMV)

- **Health & Wellness:**
  - Medical: 100% premium coverage (PPO + HSA option)
  - Dental: 100% preventive, 80% basic, 50% major
  - Vision: 100% preventive, $250 annual benefit
  - Mental Health: Unlimited therapy sessions ($0 copay)
  - Gym: $100/month stipend
  - Remote Work: $500/year home office stipend

- **Time Off:**
  - PTO: 20 days/year + 10 company holidays
  - Parental Leave: 16 weeks paid
  - Sabbatical: 1 month paid (after 5 years)
  - Sick Leave: Unlimited (good faith)

### Performance Management
- **Review Cycle:** Annual (January) + mid-year check-in
- **Goals:** OKR-based (Objectives & Key Results)
- **Process:** 360-degree feedback, manager assessment, self-review
- **Rating Scale:** Exceeds | Meets | Needs Improvement | Below Expected
- **Consequences:** 
  - Exceeds: Bonus + equity refresh + promotion consideration
  - Meets: Standard equity refresh
  - Needs Improvement: PIP (90 days), equity hold
  - Below Expected: Termination

### Termination & Severance
- **At-Will Employment:** California law
- **Notice Period:** 2 weeks (mutual consent)
- **Severance:**
  - < 1 year: 1 week per year employed
  - 1-3 years: 2 weeks per year employed
  - 3+ years: 3 weeks per year employed
  - Maximum: 12 weeks
  - Conditional on non-disparagement + IP assignment

- **Acceleration on Acquisition:** 50% single-trigger, 100% double-trigger

### Diversity & Inclusion
- **Target:** 40% underrepresented in tech by 2027
- **Recruiting:** Partnerships with diversity-focused organizations
- **Retention:** Mentorship programs, ERGs (Employee Resource Groups)
- **Leadership:** 30% women in director+ roles by 2027
- **Compensation:** Annual equity/pay audits for bias

## VI. DATA & PRIVACY GOVERNANCE

### Data Classification
- **Tier 1 - Confidential:** Customer data, financial info, trade secrets
- **Tier 2 - Internal:** Strategy docs, employee info, product roadmap
- **Tier 3 - Public:** Marketing materials, documentation, blog posts

### Data Handling Requirements
- **Tier 1:** Encryption at rest/transit, access logs, quarterly audit
- **Tier 2:** Basic security, access restricted to employees
- **Tier 3:** No restrictions, public distribution acceptable

### Privacy Policies
- **Customer Data:** GDPR/CCPA compliant, customers own data
- **Employee Data:** Standard HR confidentiality, no third-party sharing
- **Vendor Data:** NDA required, data processing agreements (DPA)

### Data Retention
- **Customer Conversations:** Customer's choice (default: 7 years, then delete)
- **Employee Records:** 7 years post-termination (legal requirement)
- **Financial Records:** 7 years
- **Transactional Logs:** 90 days (for security audit)

## XIII. CRISIS MANAGEMENT

### Crisis Response Procedures

**Scenario: Security Breach**
1. Isolate affected systems (CISO + VP Infra)
2. Notify customers within 4 hours (CEO + Legal)
3. Engage incident response firm (pre-contracted)
4. Board notification within 24 hours
5. Public disclosure per legal requirements
6. Post-mortem within 1 week

**Scenario: Key Employee Departure (VP+)**
1. CEO announces transition plan
2. Identify interim replacement
3. Backfill plan developed within 1 week
4. Board notification
5. 30-60 day transition period

**Scenario: Customer Major Outage**
1. CTO activates incident response (15 min)
2. Status updates every 15 minutes to customers
3. CEO notification at 30 minutes
4. Board notification at 1 hour
5. Post-mortem + RCA within 24 hours
