# Hercules Agent - Branding Guidelines

## Vision Statement

Hercules is the self-improving AI agent that learns, adapts, and grows with you. It's not just a tool—it's a digital partner that understands your workflows, remembers your preferences, and anticipates your needs across every platform and device.

**Tagline:** *"AI That Learns. AI That Grows. AI That Remembers."*

---

## Brand Identity

### Logo & Visual Identity

**Primary Symbol:** 🦁 The Lion
- Represents strength, intelligence, and leadership
- Conveys power, reliability, and boldness
- Universal recognition across cultures and platforms

**Secondary Icon:** ⚡ The Lightning Bolt
- Represents speed, energy, and adaptability
- Used for highlighting key capabilities and features
- Symbolizes the "self-improving" aspect of Hercules

### Color Palette

#### Primary Colors
- **Gold/Amber:** `#F7B23B` - Premium, intelligent, aspirational
  - Used for: Primary CTAs, highlights, accents
  - Conveys: Intelligence, excellence, innovation
  
- **Deep Dark:** `#07070D` - Sophisticated, trustworthy, modern
  - Used for: Primary text, backgrounds, headers
  - Conveys: Professionalism, stability, sophistication

#### Secondary Colors
- **Lightning Blue:** `#00D9FF` - Energy, capability, tech-forward
  - Used for: Secondary highlights, interactive elements
  - Conveys: Innovation, speed, adaptability

- **Neural Purple:** `#9D4EDD` - Artificial intelligence, learning
  - Used for: Accents, AI-specific features, learning indicators
  - Conveys: Machine learning, intelligence growth

#### Neutral Colors
- **Light Gray:** `#F5F5F5` - Clean backgrounds, readability
- **Medium Gray:** `#A0A0A0` - Secondary text, subtle elements
- **Dark Gray:** `#2D2D2D` - Borders, dividers

### Typography

#### Primary Font
**Font Family:** `Inter` or `Segoe UI` (fallback)
- Modern, clean, highly readable
- Weights: 400 (Regular), 600 (Semibold), 700 (Bold)
- Usage: Body text, UI elements, documentation

#### Display Font
**Font Family:** `Space Grotesk` or `Poppins` (fallback)
- Bold, contemporary, distinctive
- Weights: 600 (Semibold), 700 (Bold)
- Usage: Headlines, hero text, branding materials

#### Code Font
**Font Family:** `JetBrains Mono` or `Fira Code` (fallback)
- Monospaced, developer-friendly, clear distinction
- Usage: Code blocks, terminal output, technical documentation

## Brand Personality

### Voice & Tone

**Always:**
- **Intelligent** - Knowledgeable, expert, sophisticated
- **Empowering** - Encouraging autonomy and capability
- **Honest** - Direct, transparent, no hype
- **Innovative** - Forward-thinking, pioneering
- **Approachable** - Friendly, accessible, not intimidating

**Never:**
- Corporate jargon or buzzwords without substance
- Overly technical without explanation
- Dismissive or condescending
- Vague promises or exaggerations
- Unfamiliar slang or forced humor

### Messaging Pillars

1. **Self-Improvement** - Hercules learns from every interaction, getting smarter and more valuable over time
2. **True Autonomy** - Not locked into one platform, one model, or one workflow; you choose
3. **Deep Learning** - Understands your patterns, preferences, and goals across all your conversations
4. **Trustworthy** - Built on production-grade error handling, security-first design, and transparency
5. **Everywhere** - Works on your phone, desktop, in your favorite chat app, on your servers

## Visual Guidelines

### Layout & Spacing

**Spacing Scale:** 4px base unit
- `xs`: 4px
- `sm`: 8px
- `md`: 12px
- `lg`: 16px
- `xl`: 24px
- `2xl`: 32px
- `3xl`: 48px

### Component Styles

**Buttons**
- Primary: Gold background, dark text, bold, 8px radius
- Secondary: Dark background, gold border, 8px radius
- Tertiary: Text-only, gold color, underline on hover

**Cards & Panels**
- Background: Slight off-white or transparent dark
- Border: 1px, light gray or gold (accent)
- Radius: 12px
- Shadow: Subtle elevation (0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.1))

**Icons**
- Size: 16px (sm), 24px (md), 32px (lg), 48px (xl)
- Weight: 2px stroke
- Style: Geometric, modern, consistent with Hercules aesthetic

### Photography & Imagery

- **Style:** Clean, modern, tech-forward
- **Color Tone:** Cool to neutral, incorporating brand gold accents
- **Subjects:** Real humans using Hercules, diverse perspectives, genuine moments
- **Avoid:** Stock photos, artificial scenarios, overly polished renderings

## Brand Application

### Digital Presence

#### Website
- Hero: Large display typography, gold accent text, dark background
- Navigation: Sticky header with logo, nav links, dark background
- Buttons: Gold primary action buttons, secondary dark buttons
- Cards: Light backgrounds with subtle gold borders for features
- Footer: Dark background with gold accent links

#### Documentation
- Headers: Display font, gold color for section markers
- Code blocks: Dark background with syntax highlighting
- Callouts: Bordered boxes with gold left border and icon
- Emphasis: Gold color for highlights, bold weight for important terms

#### Social & Marketing
- Thumbnails: Dark background, gold text, bold typography
- Graphics: Include lion emoji, lightning bolt, or geometric patterns
- Posts: Mix of educational content, feature highlights, community stories
- Avatar: Lion emoji or simplified lion mark

### Product UI

#### Dashboard & Tools
- Primary actions: Gold buttons
- Active states: Gold highlight or underline
- Indicators: Lightning blue for activity, neural purple for learning
- Accent text: Gold for key metrics, purple for AI-generated insights

#### Terminal & CLI
- Brand string: Display `🦁 Hercules` with gold color
- Commands: Syntax highlighting with gold keywords
- Status: Use lightning blue for active, neural purple for AI processing
- Errors/Warnings: Red for critical, orange for warning

## Do's and Don'ts

### ✅ Do
- Use the gold color strategically for emphasis and CTAs
- Combine the lion emoji with text for immediate recognition
- Keep designs clean and modern
- Balance sophistication with approachability
- Use all capitals for acronyms (AI, CLI, SDK)
- Include the Hercules name or lion emoji in materials

### ❌ Don't
- Use other colors for primary branding (no blue, red, or green primaries)
- Distort or redesign the lion emoji
- Use outdated or overly complex design patterns
- Mix corporate jargon with the casual tone
- Over-use the brand colors (they should highlight, not dominate)
- Forget to include proper attribution or licensing
