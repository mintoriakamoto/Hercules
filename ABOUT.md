# About Hercules

## Who We Are

Hercules is a self-improving AI agent built by a passionate team of developers, researchers, and AI enthusiasts. We're not just building another chatbot—we're creating a new paradigm for how humans and AI collaborate.

Founded on the principles of **open-source innovation**, **user autonomy**, and **continuous learning**, Hercules represents what happens when you combine:
- Deep learning and adaptation
- Production-grade reliability
- User-first design philosophy
- Open ecosystem thinking

## Our Mission

**To create the most intelligent, trustworthy, and autonomous AI agent that learns from you, works everywhere you are, and respects your freedom.**

We believe that the future of AI isn't about more powerful models—it's about smarter systems that understand context, remember what matters, and improve through genuine interaction with the people who use them.

## Our Story

Hercules started as a vision: What if an AI agent could actually get better over time? What if it could work everywhere you work, remember everything you told it, and genuinely understand your goals and preferences?

Most AI agents today are stateless—they answer your current question but forget your previous context. They're siloed to one platform. They're often expensive and require significant technical setup.

We built Hercules to be different:
- **Stateful & learning:** Builds persistent knowledge across sessions
- **Platform agnostic:** Works on Telegram, Discord, Slack, your CLI, or your custom integration
- **Developer friendly:** Open source, well documented, easy to extend
- **Production ready:** Enterprise security, comprehensive error handling, proven scaling

What started as a research project has evolved into a robust, feature-rich agent that thousands of developers and power users rely on daily.
